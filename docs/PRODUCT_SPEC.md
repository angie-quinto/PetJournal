# Product Specification — PetJournal

## 1. Purpose
PetJournal provides pet owners with a reliable, private, offline-first record of their pets' health, care, weight, memories, and milestones.

## 2. Key Features
- **Multiple Pet Profiles**: Species, breed, sex, birth date precision, microchip, photo.
- **Health Records**: Vaccinations, deworming, medications, symptoms, vet visits, treatments with specialized details.
- **Weight Tracking**: Normalized weight in grams, unit conversion (kg/lbs), history, and visual charts.
- **Journal & Memories**: Milestones, observations, and photo attachments via Photo Picker.
- **Unified Timeline**: Chronological stream of health events, weight records, and journal entries.
- **Care Reminders**: One-time and recurring reminders with local notifications via WorkManager.
- **Search & Filters**: Quick search and filtering across pets and event types.
- **Backup & Restore**: Versioned JSON/ZIP export and secure local restore.
- **Veterinary Report**: Exportable health summary for vet visits.
- **Privacy & Offline**: 100% offline, zero accounts or network telemetry.
